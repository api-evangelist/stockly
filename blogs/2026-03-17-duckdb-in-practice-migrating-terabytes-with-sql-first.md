In this edition, we speak with Matthew, Data Engineer at Stockly, about his journey from Pandas to DuckDB — and how SQL-first analytics tools are reshaping modern data engineering and enabling terabyte-scale data migrations. DuckDB Data engineering is full of tools that promise performance, simplicity, and flexibility — but few actually deliver all three without friction. DuckDB has quietly become one of the most talked-about tools in the analytics world: fast, lightweight, SQL-native, and capable of querying data across wildly different sources.
